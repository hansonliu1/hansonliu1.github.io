OrthoLog

Support

OrthoLog is a personal surgical logbook for orthopedic surgery residents. There is no account to recover and no subscription to manage. If something goes wrong, tell us what you were doing when it happened so we can help.

Get in touch

  • Inside the app: Go to Settings → Feedback → Send feedback. This reaches the developer directly. Leave your email in the box if you would like a reply.
  • By email: hanson.liu@medportal.ca. Expect a reply within a few days — OrthoLog is built and supported by a practising resident.

Please do not include patient details in your message.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my cases out?

Go to History → the export button to create a spreadsheet you can hand to a supervisor. Private notes are left out unless you turn on “Include my private notes”. Spreadsheet exports never contain photos. To export images, choose the “Case book” option, which is intended for your own use.

How do I back up my logbook or move to a new phone?

If you are signed into iCloud, your logbook syncs to your own private iCloud account and follows you to a new iPhone. You can also save a backup file yourself: go to Settings → Data → Save a manual backup. Use Restore from a backup to bring it back.

Your personal backup includes your private notes. You can choose whether to include photos, since they make the backup file much larger.

Can I import an existing spreadsheet logbook?

Yes. Go to Settings → Data → Import cases from a spreadsheet.

Does OrthoLog work without signal?

Yes. Logging, searching, editing, and exporting work without an internet connection and with iCloud switched off.

How do I stop sharing usage data?

Go to Settings → About and turn off “Share anonymous usage data”. This stops collection, deletes the counts stored on your phone, and deletes what was already sent. If you are offline, the already-sent copy is removed the next time the app can reach iCloud.

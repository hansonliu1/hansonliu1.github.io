From case to logbook

Make recording part of your routine.

01 / CAPTURE

Log cases your way

Start from the body map, search for a procedure, fill in a blank form, or repeat your last case. Add a custom procedure when the catalog does not have what you need.

02 / DICTATE

Speak, review, save

Start voice logging in the app or launch it with Siri. On-device speech recognition turns your words into an editable draft. Review the suggested fields before saving.

03 / FOLLOW UP

Keep track of the details

Record surgeons, hospitals, rotations, and your involvement. Flag EPA candidates and use local reminders to follow up.

Your experience, in context

More than a procedure list.

Add the details you want to return to later, and choose the right place for them.

  • Two note boxes: a Note field that can be shared with the developer through optional usage sharing, and a Private note field that is never sent to the developer.
  • Case photos: attach pre-op, intra-op, and post-op images, each with its own note. Photos are never sent to the developer.
  • Work offline: log, search, edit, and export without a connection.

Keep patient-identifying details out of your entries and remove identifying information from images before attaching them.

Bring your logbook with you

Export, back up,
and pick up where you left off.

  • Spreadsheet export: prepare your cases for a supervisor. Private notes are excluded unless you choose to include them; photos are never included.
  • Case book export: create a personal export that can include your images.
  • Import your existing logbook: bring in cases from a spreadsheet.
  • Back up your work: sync through your own private iCloud account or save a manual backup file. The app also works with iCloud switched off.

Clear about data

Your logbook deserves care.

OrthoLog has no accounts, advertising, or tracking. Your logbook is stored on your iPhone and can sync through your own private iCloud account.

Usage sharing is on by default and can be turned off in Settings → About. It sends usage counts, custom procedure names, and the shared Note text labelled with its operation to the developer through Apple CloudKit. Private notes, photos, and voice transcripts are never sent to the developer.
